DecayMC

A simple Monte Carlo framework for relativistic particle decays and scattering, designed to emphasize physical transparency, explicit assumptions, and validation against analytic benchmarks.

  • Implemented Lorentz-covariant four-vector kinematics, two-body and three-body decays, Michel-weighted muon decay, and Rutherford scattering.
  • Validated against analytic benchmarks.

LINACLab

LINACLab is a compact research-oriented framework for relativistic charged-particle tracking and linear beam optics. It is designed to emphasize physical transparency, numerical interpretability, and consistency between particle tracking and matrix optics, rather than production-scale accelerator simulation.

  • Developed a framework for relativistic charged-particle tracking in EM fields, implementing integrators (Euler, RK4) and a relativistic Boris pusher validated against analytical tests.
  • Implemented beam-level optics including second-moment transport, RMS emittance and Twiss reconstruction, and Hamiltonian-derived linear maps for drifts and quadrupoles.
