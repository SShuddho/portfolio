Projects

DecayMC

A simple Monte Carlo framework for relativistic particle decays and scattering, designed to emphasize physical transparency, explicit assumptions, and validation against analytic benchmarks.

  • Implemented Lorentz-covariant four-vector kinematics, two-body and three-body decays, Michel-weighted muon decay, and Rutherford scattering.
  • Validated against analytic benchmarks.
GitHub Report (PDF)

LINACLab

A research-oriented framework for relativistic charged-particle tracking and linear beam optics, emphasizing transparent physics and consistency between particle tracking and matrix optics.

  • Developed relativistic charged-particle tracking in electromagnetic fields using Euler, RK4, and the relativistic Boris integrator.
  • Implemented beam optics including RMS emittance, Twiss parameters, second-moment transport, and linear transfer maps for drifts and quadrupoles.
