Research Experience

Undergraduate Thesis

Jan 2024 – May 2025 · MIST, Dhaka
Thesis

Title: Neutronics Characteristics Analysis of ALFRED with LFR Reflector Comparison and Evaluation of a Conceptual MPRR using OpenMC

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Abdus Sattar Mollah (sattar@nse.mist.ac.bd)

  • Performed advanced eigenvalue (criticality) calculations using Monte Carlo neutron transport (OpenMC), validated against standard nuclear data libraries (JEFF-3.3, ENDF/B-VIII.0).
  • Conducted full-core neutronics and burnup analyses of the ALFRED lead-cooled fast reactor and a conceptual multi-purpose research reactor (MPRR), achieving high precision (within 0.054%).
  • Evaluated neutron flux spectra, control rod worth, reflector effects, and reactivity coefficients.
  • Modeled fuel depletion, fission product poisoning, and activation for performance/safety assessment.
