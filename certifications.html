Joint ICTP–IAEA Workshop on Open-Source Nuclear Codes for Reactor Analysis

Sep 2025 · ICTP, Trieste, Italy

This intensive five-day program focused on open-source computational modeling for advanced nuclear energy systems, integrating theory, software training, and hands-on applications. It introduced Generation IV reactor concepts and the IAEA ONCORE initiative, followed by practical training in modern simulation workflows used in neutronics, thermal-hydraulics, multiphysics, and system-level analysis.

Received hands-on experience with OpenMC, OpenFOAM, Foam4Nuclear, MOOSE, and OpenModelica, including benchmarking, coupled neutronics–multiphysics simulations, and real-world reactor case studies. The program concluded with discussions on verification and validation, safety, licensing, and the role of open-source software in fission and fusion energy development, reinforced through group activities and presentations.

MTV Nuclear Engineering Summer School

Jun – Aug 2023

This multi-week lecture series provided a broad and application-oriented overview of nuclear science and radiation physics, combining fundamental nuclear theory with modern detection, imaging, and simulation techniques. The program began with core topics such as nuclear structure, nuclear forces, radiation detection, and the intersection of nuclear science with technology and policy, establishing both the physical and societal context of the field.

Subsequent weeks focused on radiation-specific domains, including gamma-ray and neutron physics, their detection mechanisms, and advanced analysis techniques such as neutron resonance transmission analysis. Radiation imaging methods, coded aperture systems, and state-of-the-art semiconductor imagers were explored alongside practical detector technologies. The program also introduced Monte Carlo methods for radiation transport, detector simulation, and nuclear data evaluation, emphasizing their role in experiment design and analysis.

The series concluded with specialized applications in fission physics, nuclear monitoring and verification, non-destructive assay, radiation protection, and advanced radiotherapy planning. Overall, the program connected nuclear fundamentals to real-world applications in security, medicine, safeguards, and energy, providing a comprehensive view of contemporary nuclear science and engineering.

CS50: Introduction to Computer Science

Jan – Dec 2023

CS50x 2023 is the online version of Harvard University’s introductory course in computer science and programming, designed for learners with or without prior experience. It teaches how to think algorithmically and solve problems efficiently, using a mix of lectures, problem sets, labs, and a final project to build practical coding skills and conceptual understanding.

The course spans core computer science concepts and multiple programming languages, beginning with computational thinking and basic programming constructs and progressing to more complex topics. Students study abstraction, algorithms, data structures, memory, and resource management, and learn to code in C, Python, SQL and to develop web content using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The curriculum culminates in students designing and implementing an independent final project that demonstrates their mastery of course concepts.

Completed 10 rigorous problem sets, 9 labs, and delivered a final project.
