Professional Bio

I am an incoming M.Sc. in Physics applicant with a B.Sc. in Nuclear Engineering (Apr 2021–Jul 2025) from the Department of Nuclear Science & Engineering at the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), graduating with a CGPA of 3.49/4.00. My work in reactor neutronics gave me a deep, practical entry point into physics: Monte Carlo neutron transport, spectral effects, and long-term isotopic evolution made it impossible to treat equations as “just engineering tools.” The more I modeled reaction probabilities and the balance between scattering and absorption, the more I became interested in the underlying principles and fundamental interactions driving these systems.

Alongside my degree, I prepared for physics graduate study through structured self-study in classical mechanics, electromagnetism, and quantum mechanics, following course pathways from MIT OpenCourseWare, IIT, and ICTP-style syllabi. In 2022, I earned a bronze medal in the International Astronomy and Astrophysics Competition, which strengthened my interest in fundamental questions.
