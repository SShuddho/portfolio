Professional Bio

I am an M.Sc. student in Theoretical Physics at the University of Dhaka, following a B.Sc. in Nuclear Engineering from the Military Institute of Science and Technology. My undergraduate research background in reactor neutronics sparked a deep interest in fundamental physics, leading me to pursue theoretical studies through both formal education and independent learning. My current research interests include particle physics, astroparticle physics, dark matter phenomenology, and physics beyond the Standard Model, with a broader focus on quantum field theory and high-energy physics. I was also awarded a Bronze Medal in the 2022 International Astronomy and Astrophysics Competition.
