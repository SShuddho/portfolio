Professional Bio

I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Theoretical Physics at the University of Dhaka after completing a B.Sc. in Nuclear Engineering from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (2021–2025), graduating with a CGPA of 3.49/4.00. My work in reactor neutronics gave me a deep, practical entry point into physics: Monte Carlo neutron transport, spectral effects, and long-term isotopic evolution made it impossible to treat equations as “just engineering tools.” The more I modeled reaction probabilities and the balance between scattering and absorption, the more I became interested in the underlying principles and fundamental interactions driving these systems.

Alongside my formal studies, I pursued extensive self-study in classical mechanics, electromagnetism, and quantum mechanics through advanced coursework and open educational resources, including curricula inspired by MIT OpenCourseWare, IIT, and ICTP programs. In 2022, I was awarded a Bronze Medal in the International Astronomy and Astrophysics Competition, further strengthening my interest in fundamental physics.

My primary research interests lie in particle physics, astroparticle physics, physics beyond the Standard Model, and dark matter phenomenology. I am particularly interested in understanding the fundamental constituents of matter, the nature of dark matter, and theoretical frameworks that extend the Standard Model to address unresolved questions in fundamental physics. More broadly, I am interested in the application of quantum field theory and modern theoretical methods to problems in high-energy physics.
